# Adani Commercial Projects

Adani Commercial Projects offers premium commercial real estate opportunities within the Adani Sports City development on the Yamuna Expressway. The commercial portfolio is designed for businesses, retailers, entrepreneurs and investors looking for modern commercial spaces in one of the emerging growth corridors of NCR.

The development focuses on Grade-A office spaces, retail destinations, business suites, commercial floors and integrated mixed-use developments.

The commercial portfolio includes multiple property categories:

Modern Grade-A office environments designed for productivity, collaboration and long-term business growth. These spaces are suitable for companies, professionals and enterprises seeking a premium business address.

### Retail Shops

Premium retail spaces positioned for visibility, customer engagement and potential footfall. These spaces can be suitable for retail brands, restaurants, cafés and customer-focused businesses.

### Business Suites

Smart and flexible business suites designed for professionals, startups and growing enterprises looking for efficient commercial workspaces.

### Commercial Floors

Flexible commercial ownership opportunities featuring contemporary architecture and efficient layouts for different business requirements.

### Mixed-Use Developments

Integrated developments combining offices, retail spaces, business hubs, dining and lifestyle experiences within one commercial ecosystem.
